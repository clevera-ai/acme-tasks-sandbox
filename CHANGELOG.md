# Changelog

## v1.0 - 2026-10-31

Initial release of Acme Tasks.

- Projects: create, rename, archive and open.
- Tasks: create, edit, delete, change status and filter by assignee.
- Settings: profile, notifications, language and team members (invite, resend, remove).
- Billing: change plan and download invoices.
- Integrations: connect Slack and Google Calendar.
