# Changelog

## v1.2 - 2026-11-01

"Invite teammates" is now "Add people"

- "Invite teammates" is renamed "Add people" everywhere.
- Add people moves out of Settings > Members into a button in the top bar that opens a dialog, so you can add someone from any page.

## v1.1 - 2026-11-01

Export your projects to CSV

- New "Export to CSV" button on the Projects list downloads all of your projects (name, colour, status, task counts) as a CSV file.

## v1.0 - 2026-10-31

Initial release of Acme Tasks.

- Projects: create, rename, archive and open.
- Tasks: create, edit, delete, change status and filter by assignee.
- Settings: profile, notifications, language and team members (invite, resend, remove).
- Billing: change plan and download invoices.
- Integrations: connect Slack and Google Calendar.
